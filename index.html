About me

Learning the way real systems get built — one deliberate layer at a time.

I hold a National Senior Certificate (NSC) and I'm actively working toward a career in IT and cybersecurity, with cloud and security architecture as the specific target. My learning style is isolated trial and error — I work through problems on my own first, testing and adjusting until I understand why something works. I don't mind group work and see real value in it, but only when everyone starts from the same foundation; otherwise the group ends up teaching itself instead of building on shared ground.

Right now that means working independently through the AWS Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) syllabus — testing real trade-offs across storage, networking, identity, and data protection myself rather than relying on surface-level definitions. I'm also open to group work, provided everyone in the group shares the same foundation — otherwise it slows the pace down rather than adding to it.

My longer-term goal is to become a senior Security Architect, working remotely for employers in the US, UK, or EU. I'm upfront that I'm early in that journey — this site reflects where I actually am, not where I'd like to appear to be.
